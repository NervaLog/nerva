# R4 demo: gateway

**Status: demonstrated.** `demo_r4` passed in CI on `main` at `0fcb4f0` (the merge of #190), 2026-11-06: [the run](https://github.com/NervaLog/nerva/actions/runs/42136037296/job/127624296815). R4's brief is [docs/briefs/r4.md](../briefs/r4.md).

- **The demo:** `zig build demo-r4`, which runs `tests/demo/r4.py` against a ReleaseSafe
  `nerva`, and the `demo_r4` job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. Both are in slice 7 (#190).
- **What it shows:** two client processes, each with its own `nerva api` on loopback beside it,
  on one three-replica cluster. A gateway with every slot taken refuses with `503 busy` and
  `"applied": false` and never cuts a request part-way. Its statsd metrics count the refusals.
  Killing one client's gateway stops only that client, and its keyed retries through a restarted
  gateway apply once. A cluster that stops answering turns in-flight writes into
  `"applied": "unknown"` and `/healthz` into `503` within the stated bound. Every error reply
  carries its code. The brief's table, "The demo", lists every step and its expected outcome.

This document grows slice by slice: what each slice ran, the cuts (with the reason), and the
recorded output of the green run.

Cuts and choices (P190): clients A and B are caller threads in the script, each group with its own
gateway process, not Python subprocesses. Step 9 asserts "unknown" for at least one in-flight
request and `"applied":false` for those still waiting to be sent, and that none of B's resends
answers 409 (as in step 8; #192). The recorded step 9 lines above predate that check. Every `join` with a timeout in the script (steps 4, 9 and 10 as well as the resend threads) is
followed by an `is_alive()` check that fails the step with one line (#200). Step 6 polls (up to three
windows) until `refused{reason:busy}` plus `queue_wait` equals the E0602 replies seen, and asserts
`displaced` is 0 (every connection the script opens sends bytes, so none can be displaced). Steps 7
and 8 tell a request cut mid-flight from a refused connect and print first-time against replayed
answers. Step 11 also checks each account's balance against the unique keys' effects, so a
transfer applied twice cannot hide in a total that stays zero. Step 5's refused drippers are
counted too.
