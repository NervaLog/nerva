# 154: R5 slice 6, `nerva backup` and `nerva restore`

2026-11-05, [NervaLog/nerva#154](https://github.com/NervaLog/nerva/pull/154).

- `src/nerva/backup.zig` renders, strictly parses and compares the backup manifest (P154).
- `nerva backup` exports and writes the manifest. `nerva restore` checks the manifest (NRV-E0808) and compares it with the export (NRV-E0809) before it imports.
- `tests/release/restore.py`, part of `test:release`, covers a backup under writes, a restore drill into a second cluster, a full restore of the wiped cluster, and every refusal.
