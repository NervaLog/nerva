# 175: R4 slice 3, `--request-timeout`, the idle probe and `/healthz`

2026-11-05, #175. `--request-timeout` (1,000 to 60,000 ms, default 30,000; NRV-E0002 outside the
range) replaces the gateway's hard-coded 30 s. After 5 s with no reply from the cluster, an idle
`nerva api` probes it with one catalog read, and a timeout turns `/healthz` to 503 E0605 (P175b).
Reconnect and probe now run before every wait, so a fast `/healthz` poller no longer keeps a broken
gateway broken (#174, P175). `check_stall_recovery` uses `--request-timeout=2000` and its bounds
held in 10 of 10 rounds.
