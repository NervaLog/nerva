# 433

2026-11-14, PR #433 (refs #418).

The signing step of `package_repos.sh` threw gpg's stderr away, so a one-off NRV-E1144 ("gpg refused the
key or its passphrase") could not be told apart from a real key or passphrase problem. #418 could not be
reproduced (3 full runs, 30 single runs of the kill test, a gpg-agent race stress: no failure), so this
change only makes the next occurrence diagnosable: the cause now ends with `(gpg: <last stderr line>)`.
`docs/ERRORS.md` row NRV-E1144 says so, and a test makes gpg fail and checks the line is carried. No
change to the signing itself. #418 stays open.
