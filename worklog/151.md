# 151: R5 slice 3, `nerva recover` on a formatted cluster

2026-11-05, [NervaLog/nerva#151](https://github.com/NervaLog/nerva/pull/151).

- `src/nerva/recover.zig` drives the vendored `ReplicaReformat` unchanged.
  - It writes `<path>.recovering` and renames it once formatted.
  - Before it starts, it probes the lost replica's own address (NRV-E0713) and a quorum of the others (NRV-E0712).
  - It times out after 60 s. Its failures are NRV-E0712 to E0716.
- `tests/recover/recover.py`, part of `test:integration`, covers recovery by repair and by state sync
  under a writer, then kills the other backup and the primary and checks that every acknowledged put
  survives. It also covers every refusal except E0716.
- P151: there is no test-only timeout flag, and `--addresses=0` is refused.
