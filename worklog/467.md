# #467 — mac-verify trusts the Homebrew tap, and says what brew refused

2026-11-15. NervaLog/nerva#467, fixing #466.

v0.7.1's release-finish passed signing and the draft's mac-verify, then `verify / macos-15` failed at
the brew step: current Homebrew refuses a formula from an untrusted tap, `brew info | jq` printed an
empty version, and the step reported "the tap has nerva , not 0.7.1", blaming a stale formula that
was not stale. The macos-15-intel job passed (older brew).

The step now runs `brew trust nervalog/tap` when `brew trust --help` succeeds (so an older brew is
untouched), and a failing `brew info`, or one with no stable version, stops at once with NRV-E1167
and brew's own words, apart from the retried "the tap has another version" case. `docs/INSTALL.md`
tells users on current Homebrew to run `brew trust nervalog/tap`. The site (`index.html`) has no
install command, so it needs no change.

Not run: `brew trust` itself (no macOS here); its form is taken from brew's own message in the log.
