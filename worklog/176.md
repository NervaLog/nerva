# 176: R4 slice 4, gateway metrics

2026-11-05, #176. `nerva api --statsd=<host:port>` sends the gateway's metrics every 10 s over UDP
(`src/nerva/api/metrics.zig`), in packets of at most 1400 bytes, allocation-free and non-blocking.
The series are: requests by route and status, refusals by reason, `unknown` replies, group size,
cluster request time by operation, queue wait, reconnects, the session and connection gauges, and
dropped lingering and statsd packets (P176). `tests/api/metrics.py` checks each against a UDP
listener, and that `refused{busy}` equals the busy replies a client saw.
