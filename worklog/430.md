# 430 — a client evicted for its release ends NRV-E0731 (#356)

2026-11-14

- `src/nerva/release_eviction.zig` records an eviction whose reason is `client_release_too_high`
  or `client_release_too_low` (from the eviction callbacks in `client.zig` and `recover.zig`) and
  logs `NRV-E0731`, so a gateway names the cause too. `diagnostic.unexpected` and `recover` turn
  `error.Evicted` into `error[NRV-E0731]` (exit 1) with both releases and the fix, instead of
  `bug[NRV-B0004]` or NRV-E0716. `error.Evicted` keeps its name; no vendored file changed.
- Tests: unit test of the recorder; `tests/release/eviction.py` (a newer release's `export`
  against a one-replica cluster), in `test:release`.
- Not covered by a binary test: `client_release_too_low` (needs a client below
  `release_client_min`), and `recover` evicted as too new. E0609/E0610 stay registered, unemitted.
- Handoff: held for the v0.7.1 freeze. Gateway `/healthz` and retry remain R6 slice 2.
