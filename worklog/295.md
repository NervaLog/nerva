and removed in ubuntu:24.04, debian:12, rockylinux:9 and fedora:latest. Decision P295. `rpm` is pinned to
4.18.2+dfsg-2.1build2 (ubuntu-24.04's).

(NRV-E1110) and the test builds the arm64/aarch64 packages from a real aarch64 build and compares the packaged
binary; the examples moved to `/usr/share/nerva`; the deb reloads systemd and both formats disable enable links
on removal; a leftover tarball install is refused (NRV-E1111); the test adds an upgrade and downgrade, the other
`package-setup.sh` branches, exact messages in every container, and `md5sums`.
