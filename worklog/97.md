# 97 — A process retries a replica address it cannot open, instead of aborting (#92)

2026-10-06

- Reported by INF-1 (Nerva on Fly): with `--addresses` naming a replica in an address family the
  kernel cannot open (`[::1]` without IPv6), the vendored message bus aborted on its assertion in
  `tick_connect` that every dialed replica holds a connection, after `init_tcp` failed with
  `AddressFamilyNotSupported`. Gateways (`nerva api`, `nerva view`, `nerva export`, the Zig
  client) aborted at once; so did replicas whose higher-index peer was in that family. Upstream
  has the same code.
- `src/message_bus.zig`: `tick_connect` counts the replicas whose socket could not be opened;
  `connect_connection` says the error once per replica (`replicas_unopened`, cleared when a socket
  opens) and dials again every tick, leaving the dial backoff to dials that reached the network.
  A gateway that never reaches its cluster now exits after its 30 s registration timeout. Recorded
  in UPSTREAM.md.
- `tests/api/addresses.py` (in `test:api`), on a host without IPv6 (skipped, saying so, with it):
  a gateway at `[::1]` says it once and exits 1 on its timeout; a cluster with replica 2 at
  `[::1]` runs on 0 and 1, which say it once, and a gateway commits through them. Both fail on the
  build before this fix.
- Verified: `check`, `test:fmt`, `test:unit` (all but the sandbox's `benchmark.perf: usage
  example`), `test:integration`, `test:api`.
