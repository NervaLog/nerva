# 179: sim-fleet `setup.sh` from a relative path

2026-11-05, [NervaLog/nerva#179](https://github.com/NervaLog/nerva/pull/179). Fixes #178.

- `setup.sh` resolves its own directory before `cd /`, so it runs as the README's "Deploy" says
  (`sh nerva/ops/sim-fleet/setup.sh`) instead of stopping before it writes `fleet.env`. Found on
  the fleet's first real deploy; the tracking issue is #177.
- The fleet now fits the host's memory. A seed of every kind peaks near 4 GB, and the first host
  (4 cores, 7 GiB) ran 3 workers, so the kernel killed seeds (137) at 3.2 to 3.9 GB before the
  unit's `MemoryMax=56G` mattered. `run.sh` defaults `SIM_FLEET_WORKERS` to min(cores - 1,
  (memory - 1 GiB) / `SIM_FLEET_SEED_MEMORY_MB`), and with fewer than 3 workers they are "mixed"
  and rotate through the kinds seed by seed; `setup.sh` writes a `MemoryMax` drop-in of
  min(56G, MemTotal - 1G). Decision P179. The reporter's handling of 137 is unchanged.
